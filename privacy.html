Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

Last updated: 1 October 2026. This policy is written to follow Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022. Template — have it reviewed by a lawyer before launch.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email address and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase and never seen by us).
  • Proposal details: the information you enter in your profile, including religion, ethnicity, caste (optional) and horoscope details. Some of this is sensitive personal data; you provide it with your consent so families can find suitable matches.
  • Photos you upload.
  • Contact details: phone, WhatsApp, contact person, birth time and place.
  • Verification images (NIC and selfie), if you choose to verify.
  • Payment receipts for Premium membership.

Who can see what

  • Approved profiles are visible only to signed-in members, never to the public or search engines.
  • Photos are visible to members or only to people whose interest you accept — you choose.
  • Your surname, phone number, WhatsApp, birth time and place are shown only to a Premium member after you both accept an interest.
  • NIC and selfie images are seen only by our review team and are deleted as soon as the check is complete.
  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Why we use it

To run the matrimony service, review profiles for genuineness, prevent fraud, handle reports, process payments and contact you about your account.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which may store it outside Sri Lanka, with industry-standard encryption and access controls.

Your rights

  • View and correct your details at any time from My Account.
  • Hide your profile at any time.
  • Delete your account permanently from My Account → Settings. This removes your profile, photos, contact details and interests.
  • Contact us to request a copy of your data or to withdraw consent.

Keeping records

Payment records may be kept as required by law. Reports about a profile may be kept to prevent the same person returning.
