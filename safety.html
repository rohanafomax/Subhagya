Stay safe

Simple steps that protect you and your family

Before you share contact details

  • Prefer profiles with the blue ID verified badge.
  • Read the whole profile. Vague details or very few photos can be warning signs.
  • Involve your family early, as is our tradition.

Never send money

Genuine marriage proposals never ask for money — not for travel, visas, customs fees, medical bills or gifts. If anyone asks, stop contact and report the profile using the ⚑ button.

When you talk

  • Speak on the phone or by video before meeting. Excuses to avoid a video call are a warning sign.
  • Do not share bank details, NIC copies or passwords.
  • Check facts: workplace, home town, family.

When you meet

  • Meet in a public place, ideally with family members present.
  • Tell someone where you are going and arrange your own transport.

Report and block

Every profile has Report and Block buttons. Reports are confidential and checked by our team. Fake profiles are removed.

In an emergency, contact Sri Lanka Police on 119.
